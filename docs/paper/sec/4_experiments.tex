\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:exp_setup}
\paragraph{Data.} All experiments use 306 portraits from Pexels (108 men, 198 women), used under the Pexels License. They are selected from a public Pexels portrait collection by automatic filters for a single, near-frontal, unoccluded color face whose apparent gender agrees with the image description, deduplicated by photographer and identity, screened visually, and cropped around the face to $512^2$.

\paragraph{Attack.} The attacker uses InstructPix2Pix~\cite{brooks2023instructpix2pix} with Euler-Ancestral sampling (50 steps, text guidance 7.5, image guidance 1.8, seed 20260812) and the four accessory instructions of FaceLock~\cite{wang2025facelock}, which yields four edits per image and defense. In \cref{sec:exp_gen}, VIBE-DistilledCFG~\cite{alekseenko2026vibe} runs with 20 sampling steps and the distilled FLUX.2 [klein] 4B~\cite{bfl2026flux2klein} with 4 sampling steps and guidance 1.0, both at $512^2$ resolution with the same four accessories phrased as additions (\eg, ``Add sunglasses to the person'').

\paragraph{Purification.} Seven standard operations are applied before editing: JPEG compression at quality 80, 50, and 30; Gaussian blur with $\sigma = 1$ and $2$; a 10\% crop followed by resizing to the original size; and a $15^\circ$ rotation. VAE-Trans~\cite{purifyonce2026}, a fine-tuned VAE encoder used as a purifier, is reported separately as the strongest purification.

\paragraph{Baselines.} The comparison includes ten additive methods: DAYN~\cite{lo2024dayn}, DIA-R and DIA-PT~\cite{dia2025}, SIFM~\cite{dong2025sifm}, DANP~\cite{zhang2025danp}, Mist~\cite{liang2023mist}, the encoder attack of PhotoGuard~\cite{salman2023photoguard}, DCT-Shield and DCT-Shield-Y~\cite{dctshield2025}, and BlurGuard~\cite{blurguard2025}. Each method is run at its native budget and, in the matched-distortion arm, with its budget rescaled so that the defense LPIPS on the eight-portrait set falls in 0.300--0.367.

\paragraph{Metrics.} The metrics are defined in \cref{sec:threat}: LPIPS, LAION, and MUSIQ for the protected image, and LPIPS, SigLIP, ArcFace, FID, and retention for the edits. Each edit of a protected image is compared with the edit of the original image under the same purification and instruction.

\subsection{Robustness to purification}
\label{sec:exp_anti}
\Cref{tab:main} reports the comparison on the 306 Pexels portraits. Before purification, the additive methods displace the edits by 0.262 to 0.415 LPIPS and \capg{} by 0.353. Averaged over the seven purifications, the displacement of the additive methods falls to 0.112 to 0.212, and that of BlurGuard to 0.275, whereas \capg{} remains at 0.364 with a retention of 1.058. The 95\% interval of this mean across the seven purifications has a half-width of 0.056 to 0.068 for the other additive methods, 27\% to 61\% of their means, 0.029 for BlurGuard, 10\% of its mean, and 0.025 for \capg{}, 7\% of its mean (\cref{tab:main}), so the displacement of \capg{} varies less between purifications. After VAE-Trans, the strongest purification considered, \capg{} retains 0.288 against 0.023 to 0.056 for the other additive methods and 0.143 for BlurGuard. The images protected by \capg{} have a LAION score of 5.343, against 5.345--5.531 for the additive methods, and the highest MUSIQ score (5.308, against 5.117--5.298).

\Cref{fig:plane}b places the matched-distortion arms on the plane of distortion and displacement. Before purification, every defense lies close to the line fitted to the additive methods at all budgets. Purification moves every additive method far below this line, whereas \capg{} remains on it. The FID between defended and undefended edits after purification shows the same separation: 44.7 for \capg{} against 17.4--26.1 for the other additive methods on average over the seven purifications, and 38.0 against 11.4--14.2 after VAE-Trans, with BlurGuard at 38.9 and 20.8.

\subsection{Distortion and displacement before purification}
\label{sec:exp_plane}
\Cref{fig:plane}a plots edit displacement before purification against distortion for the additive methods at their native budgets and at the matched-distortion arm. On the 306 portraits, the 19 points follow a single line, edit LPIPS $= 1.037 \times$ defense LPIPS $+ 0.049$ ($r = 0.968$). At the native budgets, the defense LPIPS ranges from 0.280 to 0.629, and the ranking of the methods by displacement follows their ranking by distortion. On the eight-portrait set, budget sweeps of five methods follow the same form, each along its own line ($r = 0.987$ to $1.000$). \capg{} operates at a defense LPIPS of 0.287 and an edit LPIPS of 0.353, 0.006 above the pooled LPIPS line. Before purification, the displacement of a defense is therefore determined largely by its distortion, independently of the form of the perturbation, and defenses of equal distortion are comparable.

\subsection{What purification removes}
\label{sec:exp_purify}
To separate the effect of purification on the protected image from its effect on the edit, \cref{fig:purification}a reports the fraction of the perturbation that survives a purification $P$, $\mathrm{LPIPS}(P(\tilde{x}), P(x)) / \mathrm{LPIPS}(\tilde{x}, x)$. Cropping and rotation leave most of every perturbation in place. JPEG compression, blur, and VAE-Trans separate the two kinds of defense: On the 306 portraits, Gaussian blur with $\sigma = 2$ leaves 4--16\% of an additive perturbation and 49\% of that of BlurGuard, and DCT-Shield-Y at its native budget, which places its perturbation in quantized JPEG coefficients, keeps its perturbation under JPEG compression at quality 80 (1.02) but retains 36\% and 25\% at quality 50 and 30, whereas the perturbation of \capg{} survives the seven purifications in full on average (1.057). After VAE-Trans, 83\% of the perturbation of \capg{} survives, against 3\% to 11\% of the other additive perturbations and 35\% of that of BlurGuard.

\Cref{fig:purification}b--d relate the strength of a purification to the remaining edit displacement. For the additive methods, the displacement decreases monotonically with JPEG quality, blur radius, and downscaling, and reaches \prelim{0.03--0.19}{} at the strongest settings, close to the displacement of \prelim{0.109}{} between two seeds on the same undefended image. \capg{} remains between \prelim{0.345}{} and \prelim{0.437}{} over all settings and retains \prelim{0.95--1.27}{} of its unpurified displacement; stronger JPEG compression increases it from \prelim{0.377}{} at quality 95 to \prelim{0.437}{Strength sweep on 8 portraits (\texttt{baseline/results/sweeps/ip2p/purifier\_strength/retention.csv}).} at quality 10. DCT-Shield-Y at its native budget keeps most of its displacement down to quality 70 (\prelim{0.538}{}, from \prelim{0.626}{} without purification) and falls to \prelim{0.306}{} at quality 50 and \prelim{0.190}{8 portraits, native budget.} at quality 10.

\subsection{Editors with different encoders}
\label{sec:exp_gen}
InstructPix2Pix, Stable Diffusion inpainting, and Stable Diffusion 2.1 share the same VAE encoder, so a defense optimized for one of them applies to all three. VIBE-DistilledCFG~\cite{alekseenko2026vibe}, which conditions a Sana1.5-1.6B diffusion transformer on a Qwen3-VL-2B encoder, encodes images with a 32-channel DC-AE at $32\times$ downsampling, and FLUX.2 [klein] 4B~\cite{bfl2026flux2klein} with the 32-channel FLUX.2 VAE at $8\times$ downsampling. The lower blocks of \cref{tab:main} apply the protected images of PhotoGuard, DCT-Shield, BlurGuard, Mist, and \capg{}, optimized on InstructPix2Pix at matched distortion, to these two editors without re-optimization (settings in \cref{sec:exp_setup}). On VIBE-DistilledCFG, Mist, PhotoGuard, and DCT-Shield displace the edits by 0.146 to 0.159 before purification and by 0.077 to 0.098 on average over the seven purifications, BlurGuard by 0.218 and 0.202, and \capg{} by 0.318 and 0.331, a retention of 1.069. After VAE-Trans, \capg{} retains 0.278, against 0.035 to 0.041 for Mist, PhotoGuard, and DCT-Shield and 0.103 for BlurGuard. Relative to InstructPix2Pix, the edit LPIPS averaged over the seven purifications changes by a factor of 0.91 for \capg{}, 0.60 to 0.69 for Mist, PhotoGuard, and DCT-Shield, and 0.74 for BlurGuard. The readings on FLUX.2 [klein] are \prelim{pending}{FLUX.2 [klein]: 306-portrait edits in progress.}.

\subsection{Ablations}
\label{sec:exp_ablation}
\Cref{tab:ablation} removes one component of the pipeline at a time: the optimization, by using a random $\Theta$; the Gaussian smoothing, by setting $\sigma = 0$; the angle bounds; the separation of the bounds on polar angle and azimuth; both of these; and the constraint that keeps $\|c\|$ fixed. No variant is rescaled to the distortion of the full pipeline, since the distortion each variant reaches is itself a result of its optimization. The ablation measures the protected images and their unpurified edits; purification is evaluated only for the full pipeline (\cref{sec:exp_anti}).
