\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
We used Claude Code (Anthropic), an LLM-based coding assistant, to write the code of the defense, the reimplementations of the baselines, the evaluation pipeline, and the scripts for figures and tables, following specifications given by the authors; to draft, revise, and edit the text from outlines and decisions provided by the authors; and to check the bibliography entries against the original publications. Claude Sonnet~5.5 (Anthropic) judged whether edits carry out the instruction for the rate of failed edits in \cref{sec:exp_isr}, and the authors reviewed a sample of its judgments by hand. The portrait in \cref{fig:teaser} was generated with FLUX.2 [klein]~\cite{bfl2026flux2klein}. The research question, the method design, the experimental protocol, and the interpretation of the results were determined by the authors. All reported numbers come from the experiment outputs; the authors reviewed all code, figures, and text and take full responsibility for the paper.
