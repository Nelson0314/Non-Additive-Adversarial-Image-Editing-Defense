\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
We used Claude Code (Anthropic), an LLM-based coding assistant, in three parts of this work. (i) Code: it wrote and modified the implementation of the defense, the reimplementations of the baselines, the evaluation pipeline, and the scripts that dispatch experiments to GPU servers, following specifications given by the authors. (ii) Figures and tables: it wrote the plotting and table-generation scripts that read the experimental results. (iii) Text: it drafted and revised the manuscript from outlines and decisions provided by the authors, and checked the bibliography entries against the original publications. The research question, the method design, the experimental protocol, and the interpretation of the results were determined by the authors. All reported numbers come from the experiment outputs, and the authors reviewed all code, figures, and text and take full responsibility for the content of the paper.
