\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

We compare \capg{} with additive defenses at matched distortion and measure, before and after purification, how much of each perturbation is preserved, how far the edits of the protected image differ from the edits of the original, and how often a requested edit fails.

\paragraph{Data and attack.}
\label{sec:exp_setup}
All experiments use 306 portraits from Pexels (108 men, 198 women), used under the Pexels License, each with a single, near-frontal, unoccluded face and cropped around the face to $512^2$. The attacker edits each image with InstructPix2Pix~\cite{brooks2023instructpix2pix} (Euler-Ancestral sampling, 50 steps, text guidance 7.5, image guidance 1.8, seed 20260812) under four instructions that add sunglasses, a police suit, a helmet, or a bow tie to the person, which yields four edits per image and defense.

\paragraph{Purification methods.}
Seven standard operations are applied before editing: JPEG compression at quality 80, 50, and 30; Gaussian blur with $\sigma = 1$ and $2$; a 10\% crop followed by resizing to the original size; and a $15^\circ$ rotation. VAE-Trans~\cite{purifyonce2026}, a fine-tuned VAE encoder used as a purifier, is reported separately as the strongest purification.

\paragraph{Compared methods.}
The comparison includes ten additive methods: DAYN~\cite{lo2024dayn}, DIA-R and DIA-PT~\cite{dia2025}, SIFM~\cite{dong2025sifm}, DANP~\cite{zhang2025danp}, Mist~\cite{liang2023mist}, the encoder attack of PhotoGuard~\cite{salman2023photoguard}, DCT-Shield and DCT-Shield-Y~\cite{dctshield2025}, and BlurGuard~\cite{blurguard2025}. Each method is run at its native budget and, in the matched-distortion arm, with its budget rescaled so that the defense LPIPS on the eight-portrait set falls in 0.300--0.367; on the 306 portraits, the defense LPIPS of these arms is 0.251 to 0.318 (\cref{tab:main}).

\subsection{Results}
\label{sec:exp_results}

\paragraph{Perturbation survival.}
\label{sec:exp_purify}
\Cref{fig:purification} reports the fraction of each perturbation that is preserved by a purification $P$, $\mathrm{LPIPS}(P(\tilde{x}), P(x)) / \mathrm{LPIPS}(\tilde{x}, x)$, on the 306 portraits. Cropping and rotation preserve 0.48 to 0.90 of the additive perturbations and 0.88 to 1.01 of that of \capg{}. JPEG compression preserves 0.16 to 0.65 of the additive perturbations other than BlurGuard and DCT-Shield-Y, 0.73 to 0.86 of that of BlurGuard, and 1.09 to 1.21 of that of \capg{}; Gaussian blur with $\sigma = 2$ preserves 0.04 to 0.16, 0.49, and 1.03, respectively. DCT-Shield-Y, shown at its native budget (defense LPIPS 0.629), keeps 1.02 under JPEG compression at quality 80 and 0.36 and 0.25 at quality 50 and 30. After VAE-Trans, 0.83 of the perturbation of \capg{} is preserved, against 0.03 to 0.11 for the additive methods other than BlurGuard and 0.35 for BlurGuard. These purifications act on the spatial frequency content of an image, and a mapping applied to colors alone adds no spatial structure for them to remove.

\paragraph{Edit displacement after purification.}
\label{sec:exp_anti}
\Cref{tab:main} reports the difference between the edit of the protected image and the edit of the original under the same purification. Without purification, this difference is 0.262 to 0.415 LPIPS for the additive methods and 0.353 for \capg{}; PhotoGuard (0.415), DAYN (0.363), and DANP (0.354) exceed \capg{}. Averaged over the seven purifications, it is 0.112 to 0.212 for the additive methods other than BlurGuard, 0.275 for BlurGuard, and 0.364 for \capg{}; after VAE-Trans, it is 0.023 to 0.056, 0.143, and 0.288, respectively. The SigLIP and ArcFace similarities order the methods in the same way. The perturbation that purification preserves thus still reaches the edit. The protected images of \capg{} have a defense LPIPS within the range of the additive methods (0.287, against 0.251 to 0.318), the lowest LAION score (5.34, against 5.35 to 5.53), and a MUSIQ score of 5.31, against 5.12 to 5.30.

\paragraph{Distortion and displacement.}
\label{sec:exp_plane}
\Cref{fig:plane}a plots the edit difference before purification against the defense LPIPS for the additive methods at their native budgets and at matched distortion. The 19 points lie close to a single line ($r = 0.968$), and \capg{} lies 0.006 LPIPS above it. Before purification, the edit difference of a defense thus follows its distortion, and the comparison in \cref{tab:main} is made at comparable distortion. After purification, the additive methods lie below this line and \capg{} remains close to it (\cref{fig:plane}b).

\paragraph{Edit failure rate.}
\label{sec:exp_isr}
\Cref{tab:isr} reports the immunization success rate (ISR) on 100 of the portraits: among the edits of the original image that carry out the instruction, the percentage whose counterpart on the protected image lacks the accessory, shows a different edit, or is degraded, as judged by a vision--language model with human review of 606 judgments. Without purification, the ISR of \capg{} is 17.0\%, lower than that of PhotoGuard (42.0\%; paired difference $-25.0$ points, 95\% interval $[-31.1, -18.5]$) and Mist (32.4\%; $-15.4$, $[-19.8, -10.7]$); the intervals of its differences from DCT-Shield, SIFM, and BlurGuard include zero. Over the three purifications combined, the ISR of \capg{} is 16.5\%, against 2.6\% to 5.9\% for PhotoGuard, DCT-Shield, SIFM, and Mist (differences of 10.6 to 13.9 points, lower interval bounds 7.8 to 11.0) and 12.2\% for BlurGuard ($+4.3$, $[2.0, 6.9]$); against BlurGuard, the interval includes zero after JPEG compression at quality 50. The ISR of \capg{} is thus unchanged by purification, but its absolute value is low, and most requested edits succeed for every method.

\paragraph{Editors with different encoders.}
\label{sec:exp_gen}
The lower blocks of \cref{tab:main} apply the protected images optimized against the VAE encoder of InstructPix2Pix, without re-optimization, to VIBE-DistilledCFG~\cite{alekseenko2026vibe} (32-channel DC-AE at $32\times$ downsampling, 20 sampling steps) and to the distilled FLUX.2 [klein] 4B~\cite{bfl2026flux2klein} (32-channel FLUX.2 VAE at $8\times$ downsampling, 4 sampling steps, guidance 1.0), both at $512^2$ with the four instructions phrased as additions. Without purification, the edit difference of \capg{} is 0.318 and 0.281 on the two editors, against 0.146 to 0.159 and 0.163 to 0.188 for Mist, PhotoGuard, and DCT-Shield and 0.218 and 0.231 for BlurGuard. Averaged over the seven purifications, it is 0.331 and 0.293 for \capg{}, 0.077 to 0.098 and 0.060 to 0.088 for Mist, PhotoGuard, and DCT-Shield, and 0.202 and 0.198 for BlurGuard.

\subsection{Ablation study}
\label{sec:exp_ablation}
\paragraph{Design.}
\Cref{tab:ablation} examines the pipeline on 100 of the portraits in two groups. The first group removes one component at a time: the optimization, by drawing $\Theta$ at random within the angle bounds; the Gaussian smoothing, by setting $\sigma = 0$; the angle bounds; the separate bounds on polar angle and azimuth, replaced by a single bound of $25^\circ$; and the constraint that keeps $\|c\|$ fixed. These variants are not rescaled to a common distortion, since the distortion each variant reaches is itself a result of its optimization. The second group replaces the constrained LUT with simpler color mappings whose defense LPIPS is matched to that of \capg{} on each image: a random shift of hue and saturation, an optimized global rotation of hue with a scaling of saturation, an optimized $3\times3$ affine map with offset, and a $33^3$ LUT without smoothing or angle bounds; the last three are optimized with the objective of \capg{}.

\paragraph{Role of the components.}
With a random $\Theta$, the defense LPIPS is 0.045 and the edit difference without purification 0.088, against 0.299 and 0.361 for the full pipeline. Without smoothing, both are higher than for the full pipeline (0.408 and 0.468). \prelim{Angle bounds, single bound, and luminance constraint.}{Sec.~4.2: to be written from the readings of the remaining rows of the first group, in terms of distortion, quality scores, and the edit difference preserved after purification.}
